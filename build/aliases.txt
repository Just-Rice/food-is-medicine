# slug | other names people search for | family and category tags
#
# Two problems this solves. First, the same vegetable has different names in
# different English-speaking places -- okra is ladyfinger in India and bhindi in
# Hindi-speaking kitchens, and someone searching any of those should find it.
# Second, people search by family: typing "gourd" should return the whole
# cucurbit shelf, not only the two foods with "gourd" in the USDA name.
#
# Tags are botanical families where that is what people mean (gourd, brassica,
# allium, nightshade, citrus) and culinary categories where it is not (leafy
# green, root, berry). Both are searchable and both appear as clickable chips.
