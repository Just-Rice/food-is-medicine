# A blend whose every part is in the tables gets its nutrients worked out from
# those parts by the recipe (parts by weight, ids as in data/foods.js or
# foods-more.js). A spice with no recipe, or a blend with a part no table
# lists, carries no nutrient figures at all ("nodata"): eaten by the gram, a
# spice adds little, and inventing numbers would be worse than leaving them
# out. Blend recipes are common published proportions, simplified.
